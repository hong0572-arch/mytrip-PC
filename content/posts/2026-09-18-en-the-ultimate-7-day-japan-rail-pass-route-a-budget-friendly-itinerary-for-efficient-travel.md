## The Ultimate 7-Day Efficiency Loop

To get the most value out of your JR Pass, we recommend the "Golden Route." This itinerary minimizes backtracking and maximizes your sightseeing time.

* **Days 1-2: Tokyo Arrival & Exploration**
  Start in the capital. Focus on the Yamanote line to see Shibuya, Shinjuku, and Akihabara.
* **Days 3-4: Kyoto’s Cultural Heart**
  Use your JR Pass to hop on the Shinkansen to Kyoto. Spend your days exploring Fushimi Inari and Arashiyama.
* **Day 5: Nara Day Trip**
  A quick train ride from Kyoto. Don't miss the deer park and the Great Buddha!
* **Day 6: Osaka Vibes**
  Head to Osaka for the ultimate street food experience in Dotonbori.
* **Day 7: Back to Tokyo**
  Use your final JR Pass day to zip back to Tokyo for some last-minute shopping before your flight.

**Q: Is the 7-day JR Pass worth it if I only stay in Tokyo?**
A: Generally, no. The JR Pass is designed for long-distance travel between cities like Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka. If you stay within one region, you are better off using a rechargeable IC card (like Suica or Pasmo).
