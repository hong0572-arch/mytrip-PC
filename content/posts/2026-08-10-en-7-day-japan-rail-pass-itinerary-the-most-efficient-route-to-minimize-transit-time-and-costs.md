## Master the Golden Route: 7 Days in Japan

Planning a trip to Japan can feel overwhelming, especially when you are trying to balance the cost of a 7-day Japan Rail (JR) Pass with your desire to see everything. The secret to a perfect trip isn’t rushing—it’s smart logistics. Whether you are embarking on your first **solo travel** adventure or heading out with friends, the "Golden Route" remains the gold standard for efficiency.

### Day 1–2: Tokyo – The Modern Metropolis
Start your journey in Tokyo. Focus on one side of the Yamanote Line per day to save time. 
- Day 1: Shibuya and Shinjuku for the iconic neon lights.
- Day 2: Asakusa and Ueno for traditional culture.

### Day 3–5: Kyoto & Nara – The Cultural Heart
Take the Shinkansen to Kyoto. By using a **travel planner app** like **TripMaker**, you can sync your train schedules directly with your daily itinerary. The app’s **AI Route Optimization** is a lifesaver here; it calculates the exact walking distance between Kyoto’s spread-out temples, ensuring you aren't wasting precious daylight navigating bus routes.

### Day 6–7: Osaka – The Kitchen of Japan
Finish in Osaka, just a 15-minute train ride from Kyoto. Enjoy Dotonbori’s street food and use your remaining JR Pass credits for a quick trip to Himeji Castle.
