## The Joy (and Challenge) of Solo Travel
There is nothing quite like the freedom of **solo travel**. It’s a chance to rediscover yourself, move at your own pace, and soak in cultures on your own terms. However, as a solo female traveler, the excitement often comes with a need for heightened awareness. Safety doesn't mean staying home; it means traveling smarter.

## How to Vet Your Accommodation Like a Pro
Where you sleep is your sanctuary. When vetting hotels for your next trip, don’t just look at the star rating. 

* **The Main Street Rule:** Always check the map view of your hotel. Is it tucked away in a dark, narrow alley, or is it on a well-lit main thoroughfare? 
* **Read Recent Reviews:** Look specifically for reviews from other solo travelers. Search for terms like "walkable," "safe at night," and "reception."
* **Proximity to Transit:** Being close to a metro or bus station means you don’t have to walk long distances alone at night.

1. **Share Your Itinerary:** Always keep a trusted friend or family member in the loop.
