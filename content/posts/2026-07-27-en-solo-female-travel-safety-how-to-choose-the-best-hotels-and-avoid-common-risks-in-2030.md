## How to Choose the Safest Hotels

Your accommodation is your sanctuary. When you’re traveling alone, the location of your hotel is just as important as the quality of the service. 

- **Check the Proximity to Main Streets:** Always look for hotels situated on well-traveled, main streets rather than tucked away in quiet, dark alleyways.
- **Read Recent Reviews on Safety:** Look specifically for mentions of lobby security, 24/7 front desk service, and key-card access levels.
- **Leverage Smart Filters:** Finding a hotel that balances luxury with security can be time-consuming. This is where the **TripMaker** app excels. With its specialized "Main Street Hotel Filter," you can filter your search results to show only accommodations located on safe, high-traffic thoroughfares, ensuring you never have to walk down a deserted lane alone at night.
