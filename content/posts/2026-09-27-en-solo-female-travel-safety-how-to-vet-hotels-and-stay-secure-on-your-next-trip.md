## The Joy (and Caution) of Solo Exploration

There is nothing quite like the freedom of **solo travel**. Whether you are wandering through the streets of Rome or finding peace in a Kyoto tea house, solo trips are the ultimate form of self-care. However, for many women, the excitement is often balanced with a valid concern for personal safety. 

The good news? With the right preparation and the right **travel planner app**, you can navigate the world with confidence and peace of mind.

## How to Vet Your Accommodation Like a Pro

The hotel you choose is your sanctuary. When you are traveling alone, your accommodation should be more than just a place to sleep—it should be a secure base.

- **Check the "Main Street" Rule:** Always look for hotels located on primary, well-lit roads. Avoid properties tucked away in narrow, obscure alleys that might feel daunting after sunset.
- **Read Between the Lines of Reviews:** Look for mentions of "well-lit paths," "24-hour reception," and "proximity to public transport" in recent reviews.
- **Use Smart Filters:** Manually searching maps can be exhausting. I personally use the **TripMaker** app’s "Main Street" hotel filter. It automatically hides listings in questionable areas, ensuring your hotel is always in a high-visibility, safe zone.
