## Day 1: The Eastern Hills (Higashiyama) Early Bird Special
The key to beating the crowds in Higashiyama is to arrive before 8:00 AM. 

- **Sunrise at Kiyomizu-dera:** By the time the tour buses arrive, you’ll be ready to head down to the Sannenzaka slope for a quiet morning coffee.
- **Hidden Gardens:** Instead of the main temple paths, seek out the smaller sub-temples of Kodai-ji. They offer the same serene aesthetic without the lines.

## Day 2: Arashiyama and the Hidden North
Most visitors head straight to the Bamboo Grove and leave. You’re going to do it differently.

- **Morning:** Arrive at the Bamboo Grove at 7:00 AM. 
- **Midday:** Head north toward the Adashino Nenbutsu-ji temple. It’s significantly quieter and offers a stunning bamboo path all to itself.
- **Evening:** End your trip by exploring the Pontocho area for dinner.
