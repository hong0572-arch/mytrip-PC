## The 7-Day "Golden Route" Blueprint

This route utilizes the JR Shinkansen (Bullet Train) to minimize travel fatigue.

### Days 1-3: Tokyo (The Modern Metropolis)
*   **Day 1:** Arrive in Tokyo. Focus on Shinjuku and Shibuya. Stay near the Yamanote line for easy access.
*   **Day 2:** Culture and history in Asakusa, followed by an evening in Akihabara.
*   **Day 3:** A morning in Harajuku before heading to Tokyo Station for your afternoon Shinkansen to Kyoto.

### Days 4-5: Kyoto (The Traditional Heart)
*   **Day 4:** Early morning at Fushimi Inari Shrine to beat the crowds, then explore the Gion district.
*   **Day 5:** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and Kinkaku-ji. Since navigating these temples can be tricky, this is where a reliable **travel planner app** like **TripMaker** becomes your best friend. Its AI Route Optimization automatically calculates walking distances and train schedules, helping you bypass the confusion of city transit.

### Days 6-7: Osaka (The Kitchen of Japan)
*   **Day 6:** A short 15-minute train ride takes you to Osaka. Spend the day at Osaka Castle and your evening eating your way through Dotonbori.
*   **Day 7:** Last-minute souvenir shopping in Shinsaibashi before heading to Kansai International Airport.
