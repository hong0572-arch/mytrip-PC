## Choosing the Perfect (and Safe) Hotel

Where you lay your head matters. When booking your stay, don't just look at the interior design; look at the geography. 

- **Stay on the Main Road:** Avoid hidden alleyways or quiet, dimly lit corners, especially if you plan on returning to your hotel after sunset.
- **Check Recent Reviews:** Look for mentions of staff responsiveness and lobby security.
- **Use Smart Filters:** If you are feeling overwhelmed by choice, the **TripMaker** app includes a specific "Safety & Main Street Hotel Filter." This feature helps you identify accommodations that are not only highly rated but are also situated in well-traveled, secure areas, ensuring you never have to worry about a long, lonely walk back at night.
