## How to Find the "Real" City

Finding authenticity requires a mix of curiosity and strategy. Here is how you can find the neighborhoods that don't make it into the glossy brochures:

### 1. The "Morning Commute" Strategy
Instead of starting your day at a landmark, head to a local transit station during the morning rush. Follow the flow of locals—not towards the tourist hubs, but toward their neighborhoods. You’ll find cafes that don't have English menus and parks where neighbors actually chat.

### 2. Follow the Aroma, Not the Map
Put your phone away for an hour. Walk through streets where the smell of home-cooked meals replaces the scent of overpriced souvenir shops. If you see a queue of locals waiting for street food, you’ve hit the jackpot.
