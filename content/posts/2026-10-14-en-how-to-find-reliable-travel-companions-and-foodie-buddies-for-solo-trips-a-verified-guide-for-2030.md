## How to Screen Potential Travel Buddies
When you decide to open your **solo travel** experience to others, the first step is safety. Never jump into a trip with a stranger without doing a bit of digital homework. 

- **Check Verified Profiles:** Look for users who have completed identity verification.
- **Start Small:** Don't commit to a two-week trekking expedition immediately. Meet for a "trial run" coffee or a lunch at a local hotspot.
- **Match Your Pace:** Are you a "rise-and-shine" hiker or a "late-night foodie"? Make sure your travel styles align before booking.
