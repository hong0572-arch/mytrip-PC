## Seoul in 48 Hours: Efficiency is Key

Seoul is a sprawling metropolis where ancient palaces sit next to futuristic skyscrapers. For a first-time visitor, it can be overwhelming, but with the right approach, you can capture the magic of the city in just two days.

### Day 1: The Heart of Tradition and Style
* **Morning (09:00 - 13:00):** Start at Gyeongbokgung Palace. If you wear a Hanbok, entry is free! 
* **Afternoon (14:00 - 18:00):** Wander through the Bukchon Hanok Village. The narrow alleys are perfect for photos.
* **Evening (19:00 onwards):** Head to Myeong-dong for street food and shopping.

### Day 2: The Modern Pulse
* **Morning (09:00 - 12:00):** Explore the futuristic Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP).
* **Afternoon (13:00 - 17:00):** Relax by the Cheonggyecheon Stream before heading to Namsan Park for a sunset view from the N Seoul Tower.
* **Evening (18:00 onwards):** Enjoy the vibrant nightlife of Hongdae.
