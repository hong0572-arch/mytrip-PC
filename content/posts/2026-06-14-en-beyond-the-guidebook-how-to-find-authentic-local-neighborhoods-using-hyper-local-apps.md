Finding the "real" neighborhood isn't just about walking randomly; it's about preparation. Here is how you can do it:
*   **Follow Hyper-Local Social Tags:** Look for neighborhood-specific hashtags on social platforms rather than broad city ones.

*   **Smart Planning:** Use a **travel planner app** to map out your day so you aren't wandering aimlessly after dark.
