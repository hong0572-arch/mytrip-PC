## Day 1: The Soul of Tradition and Secret Alleys

**Morning: Fushimi Inari and Tofukuji**
Start early to beat the crowds at Fushimi Inari. Instead of stopping at the base, hike further up the mountain to find tranquil sub-shrines that many tourists miss. Afterward, head to the nearby Tofukuji Temple, known for its breathtaking Zen gardens that offer a quieter alternative to the busier spots.
