## How to Choose the Most Secure Hotels

Finding the right "home base" is the foundation of **safe travel**. A beautiful boutique hotel might look perfect on social media, but its location is what truly matters.

- **Check the Proximity to Main Streets:** Always look for hotels located on well-lit, high-traffic thoroughfares. Avoid obscure alleyways that are difficult to navigate after dark.
- **The "Check-In Timing" Strategy:** Try to book your arrivals for daylight hours. If your flight lands late, choose a hotel with 24-hour reception and secure key-card access to elevators.
- **Utilize Tech Filters:** Instead of manually cross-referencing maps, you can use the **TripMaker** app. Its "Safety & Main Street Hotel Filter" is designed specifically to highlight accommodations located in well-traveled, secure areas, ensuring you aren't stuck in a secluded neighborhood.
