There is nothing quite like the freedom of **solo travel**. It’s a chance to rediscover yourself, move at your own pace, and soak in cultures on your own terms. However, I know that for many women, the excitement of a new destination is often accompanied by a lingering concern: *How can I stay truly safe?* 

After years of traversing the globe, I’ve learned that safety isn't about being afraid—it’s about being prepared. Here is how I vet my stays and navigate the world with confidence.

## Vetting Your Hotel: Location is Everything
When choosing a place to stay, the view from the balcony matters less than the street it sits on. 

- **Look for "Main Street" access:** Avoid hotels tucked away in narrow, poorly lit alleyways. You want to be on a well-traveled thoroughfare where shops, restaurants, and foot traffic are visible late into the night.
- **Check the neighborhood's "after-dark" vibe:** Search for travel blogs or forums specifically about the area. If you find multiple reviews mentioning that the area feels deserted at night, skip it. 
- **Leverage Smart Filters:** Manually searching for safe areas can be exhausting. I personally use the **TripMaker** app because it features a specific **Safety & Main Street Hotel Filter**. It automatically flags accommodations located on safe, well-lit arteries, taking the guesswork out of your hotel search.

## Navigating Like a Pro
Once you arrive, the next step is moving through the city with purpose. Wandering aimlessly can sometimes make you an easier target. 

Using a reliable **travel planner app** is a game-changer here. When I have my day mapped out, I walk with confidence because I know exactly where I’m going. I often use **TripMaker**’s AI Route Optimization for this; it calculates the best walking routes based on traffic and safety, ensuring I’m always sticking to the most populated, walkable paths between my hotel and the local sights.

## Staying Connected and Secure
**Safe travel** is all about staying connected. Even when I’m off the grid, I make sure someone back home knows my general plan. 

- **Share your live location:** Don't hesitate to use location-sharing tools. If you’re ever feeling uneasy, the **TripMaker** "Safe Mode" allows for real-time location sharing with family or friends. It’s a digital safety net that keeps your loved ones in the loop without you needing to text every hour.
- **Find your "Trip Mates":** If you want to grab dinner but don't want to eat alone in a quiet spot, try meeting up with other travelers. Through the **TripMaker** Trip Mate feature, you can connect with verified, trustworthy users in your area. It’s a wonderful way to find an eat-buddy or a photo-mate while staying within a secure community.

Planning your next trip shouldn't be a source of anxiety. By vetting your hotels, optimizing your routes, and keeping your inner circle close, you can experience the world with total peace of mind. Whether you are trekking through Europe or exploring a bustling Asian metropolis, having the right tools makes all the difference.
