## Smart Hacks for Flights and Stays

### 1. The Mid-Week Advantage
Avoid weekend flights at all costs. Booking your departures on Tuesdays or Wednesdays can often save you up to 30% on airfare. 

### 2. Embrace the Shoulder Season
Skip the July-August rush. Traveling in May, September, or October gives you better weather, fewer crowds, and significantly cheaper accommodation rates.

### 3. Use Aggregators and Price Alerts
Set up price alerts on flight trackers at least three months in advance. Patience is your best friend when hunting for budget fares.

### 4. Opt for "Main Street" Stays
Cheap hostels are great, but never compromise on location. Choosing accommodations on main roads ensures better public transit access and safer environments for late-night returns.

## Tips for a Stress-Free European Journey

### 6. Pack Light
Budget airlines in Europe are notorious for strict carry-on weight limits. Stick to a backpack to avoid hefty luggage fees.

### 7. Join Local Walking Tours
Free walking tours are abundant in major cities like Prague, Berlin, and Rome. They are a fantastic way to learn the history without a high price tag.

### 8. Eat Like a Local
Avoid restaurants right next to major tourist landmarks. Walk five blocks away, and you’ll find authentic meals at half the price.
