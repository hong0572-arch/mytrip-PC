## Day 1: The Charm of Tradition and Trendy Alleys

### Morning: Bukchon Hanok Village
Start your morning early at Bukchon Hanok Village. The traditional houses provide the perfect aesthetic for your social media feed. Aim for the "Bukchon 8th View" for the iconic shot where traditional roofs meet the modern Seoul skyline.

### Afternoon: Ikseon-dong Exploration
Head to Ikseon-dong, a neighborhood of narrow alleys filled with hanok-style cafes and boutiques. This is the heart of **safe travel** in Seoul, as these pedestrian-friendly streets are always bustling with life.

## Day 2: Modern Vibes and Nighttime Lights

### Morning: Naksan Park
Escape the crowds at Naksan Park. The old city wall offers a breathtaking view of the city. It is the perfect spot for quiet reflection or a casual photo shoot away from the tourist traps.

### Afternoon: Hangang Park Picnic
Join the locals at Yeouido Hangang Park. Whether you grab a convenience store ramen or rent a bike, it’s a quintessential Seoul experience.
