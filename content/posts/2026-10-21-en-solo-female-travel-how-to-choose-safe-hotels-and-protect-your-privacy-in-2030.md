## Choosing Your Sanctuary: Hotel Safety 101

When you're on a solo trip, your accommodation is your base camp. It isn't just about the aesthetics; it’s about geography. 

- **Check the Surroundings:** Never book based on photos alone. Use map view to check if the entrance is located on a quiet alley or a bustling main street. 
- **The "High-Floor" Rule:** Request a room between the third and fifth floors. They are high enough to prevent easy street-level access but low enough to be accessible by emergency ladders.
- **Utilize Tech Filters:** Instead of manually checking every address, I personally use the **TripMaker** app. It features a specific "Safe & Main Street" hotel filter that helps you find accommodations located in well-lit, high-traffic areas, which is a game-changer for **safe travel**.
