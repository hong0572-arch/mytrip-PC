## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How can I ensure the people I meet via a travel app are trustworthy?
Always choose platforms that require identity verification, such as the Trip Mate function in TripMaker. These tools allow you to check profiles, read reviews from other travelers, and share interests before you commit to meeting up, ensuring a reliable and fun connection.

### Is it necessary to share my location while traveling alone?
While it is a personal choice, it is highly recommended for solo travelers to share their live location with at least one trusted friend or family member. Many modern travel apps now include "Safe Mode" features that allow you to toggle this on or off, providing an extra layer of security without compromising your privacy during your solo adventures.
