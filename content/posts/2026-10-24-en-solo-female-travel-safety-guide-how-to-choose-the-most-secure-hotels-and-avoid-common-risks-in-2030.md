## Choosing the Perfect (and Secure) Hotel

When you are traveling alone, your accommodation is your sanctuary. Don’t just look for high ratings; look for location intelligence.

* **Check the Neighborhood:** Always research the area’s walkability at night. I personally use the **TripMaker** "Safety & Main Street Hotel Filter," which highlights properties located on well-lit, high-traffic thoroughfares, ensuring you never have to navigate a dark, hidden alley after a late dinner.
* **Floor Preference:** Request a room between the 3rd and 6th floors. Ground floors are prone to break-ins, while anything above the 6th floor can be difficult for emergency services to reach in certain regions.
* **The "Do Not Disturb" Trick:** Always keep the DND sign on your door even when you are out to give the appearance that the room is occupied.
