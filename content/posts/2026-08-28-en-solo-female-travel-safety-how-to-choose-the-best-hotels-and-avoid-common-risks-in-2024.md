## How to Choose the Safest Hotels for Solo Women

When booking your stay, your location is your first line of defense. Here is what you should look for:

* **Location Matters:** Always aim for hotels on main streets. Avoid accommodations tucked away in dark, narrow alleys, even if they look charming in photos. 
* **Guest Reviews:** Specifically filter for reviews from other women. Look for mentions of staff responsiveness, lobby security, and the walkability of the neighborhood at night.
* **Smart Filtering:** To take the guesswork out of it, the **TripMaker** app includes a specific "Main Street Hotel Filter." This feature highlights hotels situated in high-traffic, well-lit areas, ensuring you’re never stranded in an isolated spot after dark.
