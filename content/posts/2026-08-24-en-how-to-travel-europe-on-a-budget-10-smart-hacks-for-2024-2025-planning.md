## 10 Smart Hacks for Your European Budget Trip

- **Travel During Shoulder Season:** Aim for May, June, September, or October. You’ll avoid peak summer prices and massive crowds.
- **Master Regional Transport:** Skip expensive rail passes if you aren’t moving daily. Use budget airlines (book 2 months out) and FlixBus for incredible savings.
- **Eat Like a Local:** Avoid "tourist menus" near major landmarks. Visit local grocery stores for fresh bread, cheese, and fruit, and grab lunch specials instead of expensive dinners.
- **Free Walking Tours:** Most major cities offer "pay-what-you-wish" tours. They are the best way to orient yourself for just a small tip.
- **Walk More, Pay Less:** Skip the metro for short distances. Not only is it free, but you'll discover hidden gems you’d otherwise miss.
