## How to Discover Authentic Neighborhoods

### 1. The "Two-Stop Rule"
Most tourists never stray more than a 15-minute walk from a major landmark. To find authentic spots, hop on a bus or metro and travel two stops away from the city center. You will immediately notice a shift in the menu prices, the speed of the street life, and the general atmosphere.

### 2. Follow the Commuters
Instead of following the travel blogs, follow the people going to work in the morning. Their routines—the bakery they visit, the park where they read the newspaper—are the best blueprints for an authentic experience.
