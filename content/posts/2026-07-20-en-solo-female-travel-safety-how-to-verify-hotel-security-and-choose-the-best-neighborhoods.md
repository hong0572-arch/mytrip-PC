## Choosing the Best Neighborhoods
The foundation of a stress-free trip starts with your "home base." When choosing where to stay, do your research beyond just the aesthetic of the room.

- **Look for Activity:** Choose neighborhoods that remain vibrant after sunset. Areas with plenty of open shops and cafes indicate a high volume of foot traffic, which is a natural deterrent for trouble.
- **Check Public Transit Access:** Being near a well-lit subway or bus stop saves you from long, isolated walks at night.
- **The "Main Street" Rule:** Try to book accommodations on or near main, well-traveled thoroughfares rather than tucked away in quiet, dark alleyways.

## Verifying Hotel Security
Once you’ve narrowed down a neighborhood, it’s time to vet the hotel itself. Here is a quick checklist for your next booking:

- **24-Hour Front Desk:** Always opt for hotels with a 24/7 reception. Having someone on-site at all hours provides an extra layer of security and assistance.
- **Key Card Security:** Modern hotels should use key card systems that are restricted to specific floor access.
- **Digital Footprint:** Browse recent reviews specifically from female solo travelers. Are they mentioning the safety of the area or the helpfulness of the staff during night shifts?

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How can I tell if a neighborhood is safe before I book my hotel?**
A: Look for areas with a mix of residential and commercial spaces. High foot traffic and street lighting are key indicators. You can also use the TripMaker app’s Safety & Main Street Hotel Filter to automatically hide hotels in less accessible or poorly lit areas.

**Q: Is it safe to use public transport at night while traveling alone?**
A: It depends on the city, but generally, stick to busy routes and keep your belongings close.
