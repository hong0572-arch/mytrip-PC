Seoul is a city where ancient palaces whisper stories of the past while neon-lit skyscrapers race toward the future. For a first-time visitor, 48 hours can feel daunting, but with the right approach, it’s the perfect amount of time to fall in love with the city.

## Day 1: The Soul of Seoul

**Morning: Gyeongbokgung Palace & Bukchon Hanok Village**
Start your trip at Gyeongbokgung. If you arrive by 9:00 AM, you can catch the Changing of the Guard ceremony. Afterward, wander through the winding alleys of Bukchon Hanok Village. The contrast between traditional wooden houses and the modern skyline is breathtaking.

**Afternoon: Insadong & Ikseon-dong**
Walk over to Insadong for traditional tea houses and unique crafts. Just a short walk away is Ikseon-dong, a trendy district packed with cafes hidden inside renovated vintage buildings. 

**Pro-Tip:** Planning the transit between these districts can be tricky with Seoul’s winding streets. I personally use the **TripMaker** app to handle the logistics. ## Day 2: Modern Vibes and Night Views

**Morning: N Seoul Tower & Myeong-dong**
Take the cable car up to N Seoul Tower for a panoramic view of the entire city. Once you descend, dive into the bustling energy of Myeong-dong for some street food—the hotteok (sweet pancakes) are a must!

**Evening: Hongdae & Hangang Park**
End your trip in the youthful streets of Hongdae.
