## How to Scout Neighborhoods Using Google Maps

Google Maps is more than just a navigation tool; it is a goldmine for the curious traveler. Here is how to use it to "read" a city:

* **Look for the "Coffee Cluster":** Avoid areas with massive, branded chains. Instead, zoom into residential grids. If you see a high density of small, independent cafes and local bakeries with high ratings, you’ve likely found a thriving local neighborhood.
* **Check the "Popular Times" Graph:** Authentic spots often have a steady stream of locals throughout the day, rather than the sharp tourist-peak spikes seen near famous museums.
* **Read Reviews for Tone:** Look for reviews written in the local language or by users who mention "my favorite local spot." This is a sign you are stepping into a true community hub.
