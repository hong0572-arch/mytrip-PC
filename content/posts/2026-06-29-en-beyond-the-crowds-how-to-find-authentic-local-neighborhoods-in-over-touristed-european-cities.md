## Escaping the Tourist Bubble: Where Real Life Happens

We’ve all been there: standing in a two-hour line for a croissant near the Eiffel Tower or getting elbowed in the narrow alleys of Venice. While these landmarks are iconic for a reason, the true soul of a European city rarely hides in the gift shops. To find the authentic rhythm of a city, you need to head where the locals buy their groceries and sip their morning espresso.

### 1. Follow the "Three-Stop Rule"
Most tourists stay within the historic core. By simply hopping on a bus or metro and traveling three stops away from the city center, you’ll land in residential pockets. Look for streets with independent bakeries, local markets, and a distinct lack of souvenir postcards. This is where the magic of **solo travel** really begins—it’s just you and the city as it truly is.
