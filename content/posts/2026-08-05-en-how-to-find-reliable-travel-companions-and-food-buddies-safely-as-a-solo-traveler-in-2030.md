## The Art of Finding Trustworthy Travel Companions

The golden rule of **solo travel** in 2030 is simple: verify before you meet. With the rise of digital travel communities, it has never been easier to find like-minded explorers. When looking for a "food buddy" or a companion, focus on these three pillars:

*   **Public Verification:** Only use platforms that require identity authentication to ensure you are meeting real travelers.
*   **The "Coffee First" Rule:** Never commit to a full-day excursion immediately. Start with a quick coffee or a drink in a bustling, public area to gauge chemistry.
*   **Trust Your Instincts:** If a match seems too pushy or avoids specific details about their travel plans, it is perfectly fine to move on.
