## Escape the Hustle: Your 48-Hour Weekend Guide

Sometimes, the neon lights of Seoul feel a little too bright. If you’re craving a change of pace but only have a weekend, you don’t need a plane ticket to find paradise. From the tranquil coastal paths of Ganghwa Island to the artistic alleys of Paju, these hidden gems offer the perfect recharge.

Whether you are embarking on a **solo travel** adventure or looking for a quiet getaway, planning can often feel overwhelming. That is where a reliable **travel planner app** comes in.

## Day 1: Coastal Serenity on Ganghwa Island

Start your Saturday morning by heading west to Ganghwa Island. It’s a place where history meets the sea.

- **Morning:** Visit the Jeondeungsa Temple, tucked away in the mountains. The scent of pine and the quiet chants are the perfect antidote to city stress.
- **Afternoon:** Stroll through the Chojijin Fortress for a mix of ocean views and historical intrigue.
- **Evening:** Enjoy a local feast of grilled eel before checking into your accommodation. 

If you’re worried about navigating unfamiliar areas after sunset, **TripMaker** is an essential companion. It features a specialized "Main Street" hotel filter, ensuring you always book a stay in a well-lit, secure area so you can enjoy your late-night strolls with complete peace of mind.

## Day 2: Artistic Vibes in Paju

On Sunday, head north to Paju. It’s a haven for creatives and those who appreciate slow living.

- **Morning:** Explore the Heyri Art Village. With its unique architecture and cozy galleries, it’s a photographer’s dream.
- **Afternoon:** Grab a book at the Wisdom Forest Library, a massive, stunning space filled with floor-to-ceiling shelves.
- **Evening:** Wrap up your trip at a riverside cafe overlooking the Imjin River as the sun sets.
