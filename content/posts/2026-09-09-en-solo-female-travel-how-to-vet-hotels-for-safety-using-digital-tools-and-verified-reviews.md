## Master the Art of Solo Female Travel
There is a unique kind of freedom that comes with **solo travel**. It’s just you, your camera, and the world—but as any seasoned traveler knows, your independence should never come at the expense of your security. Finding a sanctuary after a long day of exploring is the most important part of your **travel planning**. Today, I’m sharing how I vet hotels to ensure I stay safe while exploring the globe.

## How to Vet Your Stay Like a Pro

### 1. Dig Beyond the Star Rating
When looking at reviews, don’t just look at the overall score. Use the "Search Reviews" feature on booking sites to look for keywords like "safe," "walkable," "lighting," and "staff." If you see a pattern of complaints regarding late-night entry or dark surroundings, cross it off your list immediately.

### 2. Prioritize "Main Street" Locations
A hotel might look stunning in photos, but if it's tucked away in a dimly lit alley, it’s a no-go. You want a property located on a well-trafficked thoroughfare. This is where the **TripMaker** app becomes your best friend. With its "Safety & Main Street Hotel Filter," you can specifically search for accommodations located in areas known for being well-lit and accessible, saving you the stress of guessing if a neighborhood is safe to walk through after dark.

### 3. Leverage Real-Time Tech
Once you’ve booked your stay, technology remains your best shield. I always recommend using a dedicated **travel planner app** that keeps your itinerary, maps, and communication in one place. **TripMaker** takes this a step further by offering "Safe Mode," which allows you to share your real-time location with trusted friends or family, ensuring your loved ones know exactly where you are during your **safe travel** adventures.

The secret to enjoying your trip isn't just about where you go, but how you get there.
