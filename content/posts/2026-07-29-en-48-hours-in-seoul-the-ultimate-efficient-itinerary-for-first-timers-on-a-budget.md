## Welcome to Seoul: A 48-Hour Whirlwind

Seoul is a city where neon-lit skyscrapers sit comfortably beside centuries-old palaces. For a first-timer on a budget, it can feel overwhelming—but it doesn’t have to be. With a bit of planning, you can soak in the history, the food, and the vibes without breaking the bank.

### Day 1: Tradition Meets Modernity

Start your morning at Gyeongbokgung Palace. If you arrive early, you can catch the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony for free. Afterward, wander through Bukchon Hanok Village, where the traditional architecture offers the perfect backdrop for photos. 

By midday, you’ll want to head toward Insadong for affordable, authentic Korean street food like *hotteok* (sweet pancakes) or *tteokbokki* (spicy rice cakes). 

As you navigate the winding alleys, you might find yourself checking maps constantly. This is where **TripMaker** shines. Instead of wasting time on transit, use its AI Route Optimization feature. ### Day 2: The Pulse of the City

Dedicate your second day to the vibrant energy of Myeong-dong and the serenity of Namsan Park. While Myeong-dong is a shopper’s paradise, keep your budget in check by sticking to the incredible variety of food stalls rather than high-end boutiques.
