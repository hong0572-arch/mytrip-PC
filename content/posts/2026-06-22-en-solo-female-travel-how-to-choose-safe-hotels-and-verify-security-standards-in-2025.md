## How to Choose the Perfect (and Safe) Hotel

When traveling alone, your accommodation is your sanctuary. Don’t just look at the star rating; look at the logistics.

*   **The Main Street Rule:** Always prioritize hotels located on well-lit, high-traffic thoroughfares. Avoid obscure alleyways, regardless of how "charming" they look in photos. 
*   **24/7 Staff Presence:** A front desk that is staffed around the clock acts as a primary layer of security. Avoid self-check-in properties if you feel uneasy about being the only one with access to the building.
