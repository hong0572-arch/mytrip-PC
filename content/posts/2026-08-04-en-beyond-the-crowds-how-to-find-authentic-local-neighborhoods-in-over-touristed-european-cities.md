## Step 1: Follow the "Three-Stop Rule"
Most tourists stay within a two-kilometer radius of the main train station or cathedral. To find the "real" city, look at a map and travel three stops away from the city center via the local tram or metro. These peripheral neighborhoods are often where the best local markets and cozy cafes hide.
