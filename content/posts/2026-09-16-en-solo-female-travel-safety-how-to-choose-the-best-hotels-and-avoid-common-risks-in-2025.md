## Choosing the Right Accommodation
Your hotel is your sanctuary. When you are on a **solo travel** adventure, the location of your stay is far more important than the room aesthetics.

* **Prioritize Main Street Locations:** Always look for hotels that are directly accessible from main, high-traffic roads. Avoid alleys or poorly lit side streets that might be deserted at night.
* **The "Safe Neighborhood" Filter:** If you’re unsure about an area, leverage tech to your advantage. Tools like **TripMaker** feature a specialized "Main Street Hotel Filter," which narrows down your search to properties located in secure, highly-trafficked areas, ensuring you never have to walk down a dark street alone.
* **Check Verified Reviews:** Look for reviews specifically from other solo female travelers. If they mention the staff’s responsiveness and the security of the building entrance, that’s a green flag.
