## The Perfect 48-Hour Kyoto Itinerary

### Day 1: The Classic Icons
- **Morning:** Start early at Fushimi Inari Shrine to beat the crowds. The hike up takes about 2 hours—worth every step for the view.
- **Afternoon:** Head to Kiyomizu-dera. Wander through the historic Higashiyama streets for authentic matcha sweets.
- **Evening:** Explore Gion at dusk. If you’re lucky, you might spot a Geiko darting between wooden townhouses.

### Day 2: Nature and Serenity
- **Morning:** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Pro tip: Arrive by 7:30 AM to capture the stillness.
- **Afternoon:** Kinkaku-ji (The Golden Pavilion) followed by the peaceful Ryoan-ji rock garden.
- **Evening:** Enjoy a local Izakaya dinner near Pontocho Alley.
