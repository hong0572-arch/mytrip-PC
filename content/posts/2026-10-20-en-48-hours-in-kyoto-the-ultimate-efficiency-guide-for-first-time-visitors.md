## Day 1: The Classic Icons
Start your journey early to beat the crowds at Fushimi Inari-taisha. 

*   **Morning:** Hike the thousands of vermilion torii gates.
*   **Lunch:** Head toward Gion for authentic Yudofu (tofu hot pot).
*   **Afternoon:** Explore Kiyomizu-dera. The view of the city from the wooden stage is breathtaking.
*   **Evening:** Wander the narrow alleys of Pontocho.
