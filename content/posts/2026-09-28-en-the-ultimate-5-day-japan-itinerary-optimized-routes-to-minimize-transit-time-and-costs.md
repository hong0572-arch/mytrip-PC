## The Optimized 5-Day "Golden Route"

To minimize transit costs and time, we recommend sticking to the Tokyo-Kyoto axis. This avoids excessive domestic flights and keeps you moving efficiently through the heart of Japan.

### Day 1-2: Tokyo Energy
Start in Tokyo. Focus on one district per day—for example, Shibuya and Harajuku on Day 1, and Asakusa and Ueno on Day 2. By grouping nearby neighborhoods, you save significantly on subway fares.

### Day 3-5: The Cultural Calm of Kyoto
Take the Shinkansen early on Day 3. Spend your time in the Higashiyama district and Fushimi Inari. By staying central, you can walk to many historical sites rather than relying on constant taxi services.
