To maximize your pass, focus on the high-speed Shinkansen corridors:

* **Days 1-2: Tokyo Arrival.** Explore Shinjuku and Shibuya. Don't worry about getting lost in the maze-like stations; use **TripMaker** to manage your arrival points effortlessly.
* **Days 3-4: Kyoto Culture.** Use your JR Pass to jump on the Shinkansen to Kyoto. Spend these days exploring Fushimi Inari and Gion.
