## Day 1: Tradition and Tranquility

Start your morning at Fushimi Inari Shrine before the crowds arrive. By heading there at sunrise, you capture the true spirit of the vermillion gates. 

* **Morning:** Fushimi Inari and Tofuku-ji.
* **Afternoon:** Head to the Higashiyama district. Walking through Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka feels like stepping back in time.
* **Evening:** Explore the Gion district.

## Day 2: Nature and Modern Culture

Spend your second day in the western part of the city. Start with the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Afterward, head toward the Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji).

* **Morning:** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and Tenryu-ji Temple.
* **Afternoon:** Kinkaku-ji and the Daitoku-ji area.
* **Evening:** Pontocho Alley for dinner.
