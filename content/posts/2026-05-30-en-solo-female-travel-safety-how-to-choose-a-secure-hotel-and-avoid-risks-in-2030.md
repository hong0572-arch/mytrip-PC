## How to Choose a Secure Hotel for Solo Travel

Your choice of accommodation is the foundation of your safety. When browsing for a place to stay, don't just look for aesthetic decor; focus on the logistics of the location.

*   **Prioritize Main Street Visibility:** Avoid hotels tucked away in dark alleys or secluded residential blocks. Hotels situated on main thoroughfares are naturally more secure due to constant pedestrian traffic and better street lighting.
*   **Check Verified Reviews for Solo Travelers:** Look for feedback specifically mentioning lobby security, elevator access control, and staff responsiveness to late-night check-ins.
*   **Digital Security:** Opt for accommodations that offer mobile key access, reducing the risk of lost physical keys or unauthorized duplicate card creation.

To take the guesswork out of this process, the **TripMaker** app includes a dedicated "Main Street" hotel filter. It automatically prioritizes listings located in well-trafficked, secure areas, ensuring you’re never stranded in an isolated spot after dark.

Even with a secure home base, how you navigate the city matters. Constant vigilance is great, but relying on the right **travel planner app** can help reduce your mental load.

1.  **Map Your Route in Advance:** Use AI-powered tools to verify your walking routes. Avoid taking shortcuts through unverified paths or empty parks, especially after sunset.
2.  **Stay Connected:** Always keep your phone charged and utilize real-time location sharing. Tools like the **TripMaker** Safe Mode allow you to share your live movements with trusted friends or family, providing peace of mind for both you and your loved ones back home.
3.  **Opt for Public Transit or Verified Cabs:** If you’re unsure about the safety of an area, utilize local ride-hailing services rather than walking late at night.
