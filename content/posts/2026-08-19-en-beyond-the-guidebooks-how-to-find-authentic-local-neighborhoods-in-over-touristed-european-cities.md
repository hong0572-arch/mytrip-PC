## How to Find the "Real" Neighborhoods

The secret to a memorable trip is stepping outside the radius of the "Must-See" list. Here is how you can find authentic vibes:

- **Follow the Commuter Flow:** Don't just look for sights; look for the local lifestyle. Find neighborhoods that are two or three metro stops away from the city center, where you see locals heading to work in the morning or gathering at parks in the evening.
- **Seek Out Local Markets:** Skip the tourist flea markets. Ask your hotel staff or Airbnb host where *they* go for groceries. These areas are usually surrounded by authentic cafes and family-run bakeries.
- **Ditch the Rigid Itinerary:** Sometimes the best discoveries happen by accident. If you’re worried about getting lost or planning your day on the fly, **TripMaker** offers AI Route Optimization. It automatically calculates the best walking routes based on real-time traffic, allowing you to explore hidden alleys without the stress of getting turned around.
