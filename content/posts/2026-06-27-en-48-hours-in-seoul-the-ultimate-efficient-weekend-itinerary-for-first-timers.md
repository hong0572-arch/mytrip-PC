## Welcome to Seoul: Your Perfect Weekend Awaits

Seoul is a city where ancient palaces stand in the shadow of neon-lit skyscrapers. For a first-timer, the sheer scale can be overwhelming, but with a solid plan, you can experience the magic without the stress. Whether you are embarking on a **solo travel** journey or exploring with friends, efficiency is the secret to a great trip.

## Day 1: Tradition Meets Modernity

**Morning: Gyeongbokgung Palace & Bukchon Hanok Village**
Start your trip at Gyeongbokgung Palace. If you arrive early, you can catch the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony. Afterward, take a short stroll to Bukchon Hanok Village to wander through traditional alleyways. 

**Afternoon: Insadong and Ikseon-dong**
Head to Insadong for authentic tea houses and crafts. Just a few blocks away, Ikseon-dong offers a trendy maze of renovated Hanok cafes. Navigating these winding alleys can be tricky, which is why I always recommend using the **TripMaker** app. Its AI Route Optimization feature automatically calculates the best walking paths, ensuring you spend more time sipping latte and less time checking maps.

## Day 2: The Pulse of the City

**Morning: N Seoul Tower & Myeongdong**
Take the cable car up to N Seoul Tower for a panoramic view of the city. Descend into the bustling streets of Myeongdong, a hub for skincare lovers and street food enthusiasts.

**Afternoon: Hongdae & Hangang Park**
Finish your trip in Hongdae, the heart of youth culture. If you’re a **solo travel** enthusiast looking for company, the Trip Mate feature on **TripMaker** is a game-changer.
