## Why Skip the Tourist Hotspots?

We’ve all been there: standing in a two-hour line for a mediocre café just because it’s "the place" on Instagram. But the true soul of a city—the neighborhood bakery where locals grab their morning sourdough, or the quiet park where neighbors play chess—is usually found just a ten-minute walk away from the chaos. Finding these spots is the best part of **solo travel**, but it can be intimidating when you aren't sure where to start.

## How to Find Authentic Neighborhoods

### 1. Follow the "Three-Street Rule"
When visiting major cities, most tourists stay within a three-block radius of the main attractions. To find the real pulse of a city, walk at least three streets past the "tourist zone." Look for local grocery stores, dry cleaners, or primary schools. If you see locals running errands, you’ve found the neighborhood pulse.
