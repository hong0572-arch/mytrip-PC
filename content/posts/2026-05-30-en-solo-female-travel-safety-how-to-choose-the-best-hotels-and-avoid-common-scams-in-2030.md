## Choosing the Best Hotels for Peace of Mind

When you're traveling solo, your accommodation isn't just a place to sleep—it’s your home base. A common mistake is prioritizing price over location. For female travelers, visibility is your best friend.

- **Check the Path:** Before booking, look for properties situated on main thoroughfares or near public transit hubs. Avoid hotels tucked away in isolated backstreets, especially if you expect to arrive after dark.

## Dodging Common Scams in 2030

Scams evolve with technology, but the "friendly stranger" tactic remains a classic. Be wary of unsolicited help at train stations or people claiming to be "official" tourism staff without proper identification.

To keep your **safe travel** journey on track, always use verified transport services and consult your **travel planner app** to check real-time routes before following any "shortcuts" suggested by strangers.
