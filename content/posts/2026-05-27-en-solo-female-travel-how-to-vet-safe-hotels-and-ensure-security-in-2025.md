Choosing the right base is the most critical step in your planning. When booking a hotel, don’t just look at the star rating or the breakfast menu. Use these practical vetting strategies:

* **Location is Everything:** Always check if your hotel is situated on a main road or a well-populated thoroughfare. Avoid properties tucked away in dark, narrow alleyways.
* **Read Recent Reviews Specifically for "Solo":** Filter reviews to see what other solo female travelers have said. Specifically, look for mentions of "well-lit entrance," "24-hour reception," and "neighborhood feel at night."
