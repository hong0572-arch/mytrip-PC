## How to Vet Hotels Like a Pro

The place you rest your head is your sanctuary, so choosing the right accommodation is non-negotiable. Don’t just look at the star rating; look at the geography.

- **Check the "Main Street" Factor:** Hotels tucked away in quiet, dark alleyways might seem charming, but they are difficult to navigate alone at night. Always look for properties located on main thoroughfares. If you are struggling to visualize the area, **TripMaker** simplifies this process with its "Main Street Hotel Filter," which specifically identifies hotels in high-visibility, well-trafficked areas.
- **Read Between the Lines of Reviews:** When reading guest reviews, specifically filter for comments about "night staff" and "area accessibility." If previous solo travelers mention feeling comfortable walking back late at night, that’s a green flag.
- **Request Higher Floors:** While not a dealbreaker, requesting a room above the ground floor can offer an extra layer of privacy and security.
