## Empower Your Adventure: Solo Female Travel Safety

There is nothing quite as liberating as embarking on your first solo trip. The world opens up in a new way when you are the captain of your own itinerary. However, as any experienced traveler knows, **solo travel** requires a bit of extra preparation, especially when it comes to choosing where you lay your head at night. 

Your choice of accommodation is the foundation of your safety. Here is how to evaluate your hotel like a pro.

## How to Assess Hotel Safety Before You Book

### 1. Check the Neighborhood Context
A hotel might have four stars, but is it located down a dark, isolated alley? Always use Google Street View to look at the immediate surroundings. You want to see high foot traffic, open shops, and good lighting. If you find this overwhelming, the **TripMaker** app includes a specialized "Main Street Hotel Filter," which automatically prioritizes accommodations located in well-traveled, safe zones, giving you peace of mind before you even book.

### 2. Dive Deep Into Recent Reviews
When reading reviews, filter by "Solo Traveler" or "Recent." Look for mentions of:
- **Staff attentiveness:** Do they acknowledge guests at the front desk?
- **Key card security:** Does the elevator require a key card to access guest floors?
- **Late-night arrivals:** Do reviewers mention feeling safe walking back after dark?

### 3. Safety Features to Look For
* **24-hour reception:** This is non-negotiable for solo female travelers.
* **In-room safes:** Essential for your passport and extra cash.
* **Well-lit common areas:** Avoid hotels with dark, labyrinthine hallways.
