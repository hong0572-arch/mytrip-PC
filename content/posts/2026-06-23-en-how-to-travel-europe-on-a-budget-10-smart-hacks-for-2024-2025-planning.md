## How to Stretch Your Euros Further

### 1. Master the "Shoulder Season"
Skip the peak summer months (July–August). Traveling in May, June, September, or October offers thinner crowds and significantly lower prices on flights and boutique stays.

### 2. Prioritize Free Walking Tours
Almost every major European city offers "free" walking tours. They are a fantastic way to orient yourself, learn local history, and meet fellow travelers. Just remember to tip your guide!

### 3. Use Local Transport Passes
Avoid expensive taxis. Most European cities have efficient, affordable public transport networks. Buying a 24 or 72-hour city pass can save you a fortune on trains and buses.

### 5. Eat Like a Local
Avoid restaurants right next to major tourist landmarks. Walk three blocks away, and you’ll find the same authentic pasta or tapas for half the price.

### 6. Book Accommodation in Safe Zones
