The biggest concern for many travelers is where to lay their head at night. A beautiful hotel room means little if the neighborhood feels isolating after dark.

*   **Check the "Main Street" Factor:** Always use Google Maps Street View to "walk" the block surrounding your potential hotel. Are there businesses open late? Is the lighting adequate?
*   **The "Verified Reviews" Deep Dive:** When reading reviews, specifically filter for terms like "walkable at night" or "well-lit entrance."
